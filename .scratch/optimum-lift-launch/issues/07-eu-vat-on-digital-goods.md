# EU VAT on digital goods, and who absorbs it

Type: research
Status: open
Blocked by: 02

## Question

Selling digital downloads from Kosovo (non-EU) to consumers in EU member states triggers VAT in the buyer's country, typically via the non-Union OSS scheme. What exactly is the obligation, and does the chosen payment rail absorb it?

Merchants of Record generally handle VAT entirely — that is much of what their higher fee buys. A plain gateway leaves the whole obligation with the seller: registration, collection at the correct per-country rate, and filing.

Also: what applies to sales *within* Kosovo, and to Switzerland (non-EU, its own rules) — a significant diaspora market.

## Definition of done

A clear statement of what must be collected, registered, and filed, and by whom. The answer feeds directly into pricing: EUR 7.99 is either VAT-inclusive or not, and that changes net revenue per sale by roughly 20%.
