# Lock the domain and brand

Type: task
Status: open

## Question

Is `optimumlift.com` available, and what is actually registered?

Check `.com` first, then regional alternatives (`.eu`, `.al`, `.xyz`, `optimumlift.shop`). If the exact brand is taken by an active fitness business, that is a genuine problem worth knowing about now rather than after a logo exists — the brand carries goodwill from 600+ physical sales, so renaming is expensive.

## Definition of done

A domain is registered and owned. The answer records what was registered and where, plus any trademark or existing-business collision found.

Unblocked — do this today. It is a few minutes of work that constrains a hard-to-reverse decision, and every day it waits is a day someone else can take the name.
