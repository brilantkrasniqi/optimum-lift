# Product page for cold Meta traffic

Type: prototype
Status: open

## Question

What does the product page look like, given that nearly every visitor arrives from a Meta ad, has never heard of Optimum Lift, and is being asked to type card details into an unfamiliar site for the first time?

This page *is* the business. Acquisition is entirely paid, so conversion rate on this one page decides whether the ad spend works.

The hard part is not layout, it is **trust**. Card entry is the real friction in a market used to cash on delivery. So the page has to answer: who is this person, why should I believe this Plan works, what exactly do I get, and is it safe to pay here — before it asks for money.

## Definition of done

A rough, concrete artifact to react to: the page's sections, in order, with the trust-building elements placed deliberately. Not production code.

## Notes

An `html.example` for the home and product pages will be supplied. **Ask for it when starting this ticket** — reacting to that design is far more useful than inventing one, and it is a stated input to the project.

Use social proof that actually exists: 600+ people have bought from this brand. That is a real number and it belongs on the page.
