# Choose the payment rail and open the account

Type: task
Status: open
Blocked by: 01, 11

## Question

Pick one rail from the research and get a live merchant account.

This is the critical path of the entire map: until money can move, nothing else on it matters. There is a registered Kosovo business, which unlocks most serious options — but expect KYC, a business bank account, and a lead time measured in weeks rather than hours.

## Definition of done

A merchant account that can accept a card payment from an EU buyer, with credentials stored somewhere the shop can use them. The answer records: which rail, what it cost to set up, actual fee structure, payout method and timing, and anything discovered during signup that later tickets depend on.
